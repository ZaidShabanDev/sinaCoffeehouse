# SINA Coffee (@sinacoffeejo) — Design & Brand Breakdown

> Specialty coffee house in Amman, Jordan. Tagline: *"An oasis in the heart of Amman, Blending Comfort and Nature."*
> Arabic name: **سِينا | للقهوة المُختصة** (Sina | for specialty coffee)
>
> Note: everything below comes from looking at their Instagram profile and grid. Font names are **closest matches I can see**, not confirmed. Hex colors are eyeballed estimates.

---

## 1. Brand Concept in One Line

**Desert heritage meets modern café.** Camels, tatreez embroidery, a dallah coffee pot and Arabic calligraphy on one side; clean minimal cups, kraft bags, editorial serif type and lifestyle photography on the other. The tagline's "oasis" idea comes through as green plants, warm light and natural textures.

---

## 2. Logo System

### Primary mark: the striped camel
- A **camel head and neck drawn only with flowing parallel lines**, like zebra or wood-grain stripes, with no solid outline.
- Thin, even-weight lines in **rust/cinnamon brown** on cream.
- Carries a small **® mark** at the top right.
- Used as the profile picture (cream circle), on the menu wall, on staff t-shirts (white on black) and as a sticker or graphic.
- Feel: hand-drawn and organic, like an engraving. It's the strongest piece of the identity.

### Arabic wordmark: سِينا
- **Calligraphic hand-lettering** with a long, swooping *kashida* (stretched baseline) under the س.
- Includes harakat (diacritics), such as the kasra under the س, which adds a traditional feel.
- Shown in rust brown on cups, kraft bags and a **cross-stitched (tatreez) framed version**.

### Latin wordmark: SINA
- All caps, **quirky high-contrast display serif** with curved, flared strokes. The **N has a curved diagonal**, almost Art-Nouveau or 70s in style.
- Used on the backlit exterior sign (warm halo lighting behind the letters) and the terrace sign.
- The Instagram display name uses **wide tracking**: `S I N A`.
- Closest fonts: *Gloock, PP Editorial Old, Ogg, Migra, Romie*.

## 4. Typography in Posts

| Use | Style | Closest matches |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic headlines (e.g. صباحيات سينا، أفوكادو و بيض) | **Thin modern Arabic sans** with heavy **tatweel stretching** (ـــ) between letters for an airy, spaced-out look. Usually white over photos. | *IBM Plex Sans Arabic Light, Tajawal Light, Alexandria Thin* |
| Arabic display (Eid post: أضحى مبارك) | Large **calligraphic/brush lettering** in rust brown, matching the wordmark | Custom or *Aref Ruqaa, Reem Kufi Ink*-style |
| English headlines ("SUNSET AT SINA", "Todays Vibe:") | **Tall, condensed, high-contrast serif**, either in all caps or title case | *Playfair Display (condensed), Bodoni Moda, PP Editorial New, Instrument Serif* |
| English accents ("Chocolate Cake") | **Italic serif**, small, used as a label | *Instrument Serif Italic, Cormorant Italic* |
| English sub-lines ("iced drinks, lots of ice.") | Small **lowercase sans**, casual, ends with a period | *Inter, Helvetica Neue* |
| Asides ("\*Work can wait edition\*") | Small sans wrapped in **asterisks** for a wink | — |
| Menu board | **Condensed monoline caps** with wide-ish tracking, olive ink; prices formatted as `3.50 JOD` | *Oswald Light, DIN Condensed, Bebas Neue Book* |

**Type pattern:** a big serif (EN) or stretched thin Arabic (AR) headline, plus a tiny lowercase sans sub-line. Text is almost always **white, centered and laid directly on the photo**, with no boxes, shadows or stickers.

## 5. Iconography (Story Highlights)

- **Cream circles** with **thin rust-brown line icons**: a hot cup next to an iced drink (Drinks Menu), a fried-egg breakfast plate (Breakfast Menu), and a stopwatch (Working Hours).
- **Solid rust camel silhouettes** for "Our People".
- One consistent stroke weight, rounded and slightly hand-drawn. This matches the logo's line style.

---

## 6. Cultural / Heritage Motifs

- **Tatreez (Palestinian/Jordanian cross-stitch):** the logo stitched on canvas in a wooden frame, red embroidered textiles in packaging, and a **tatreez border strip** (olive/yellow) running along the menu wall.
- **Camel** as the mascot, repeated in the logo, the icons and the Eid illustration.
- **Vintage engraving illustration** (Eid al-Adha post): a crosshatched camel and sheep, a **dallah** (Arab coffee pot) and patterned floor tiles, all in brown ink on cream. It reads like an old print or etching.
- **Bilingual identity**: Arabic is often given top billing over English.

---

## 7. Photography Style

- **Warm, natural and slightly filmic.** Golden-hour light, soft shadows and a little grain, like a muted warm preset.
- **Close-ups of texture:** latte art in a cortado glass, macro shots of roasted beans, a slow-motion swirl of milk into iced coffee, matcha poured over milk.
- **Food is shot top-down or at 45°** on grey/black stoneware or wooden boards (avocado toast, eggs, hash browns, pancakes, salads).
- **Candid lifestyle shots:** people in denim, headphones, sunglasses; low-angle shots of someone sitting on a bentwood chair; people working on laptops.
- **Moody night shots:** a backlit sign, warm interior glow, dark surroundings.
- **Desert / outdoors:** a person drinking iced coffee next to a camel against a blue sky, which reinforces the camel story.
- Almost every post is a **Reel**, cropped to 4:5 in the grid.

---

## 8. Space & Interior (as seen in posts)

- **Outdoor terrace** with a modern **pergola / slatted roof**, stone facade and lots of **potted greenery** (the "oasis" idea).
- **Bentwood (Thonet-style) wooden chairs**, small round bistro tables and wooden trays.
- **Rough white stucco / textured plaster walls** paired with **grey stone or concrete counters**.
- Large **glass facade** for daylight; warm **pendant and sconce lighting** at night.
- Menu painted or printed **directly on the wall**, with the camel logo and a tatreez trim.

---

## 9. Packaging & Merch

- **Kraft paper bags** with the Arabic calligraphic wordmark printed in a single color.
- **Clear plastic cold cups** with the rust Arabic wordmark, so the drink colors (matcha, iced latte) do the visual work.
- **Black staff t-shirts** with the white camel logo and a small caption ("a person who works with…").
- **Gift boxes** with tatreez-patterned textiles and red details.

---

## 10. Copy & Tone of Voice

- **Bilingual and playful**, mixing Jordanian colloquial Arabic and casual English.
  - "زاكي بس أزكى" — tasty, but tastier
  - "يومك كان طويل" — your day was long
  - "مسويات — *Work can wait edition*"
  - "صباحيات سينا" — Sina mornings
- **Recurring series format:** "Todays Vibe:" + a short line (e.g. *iced drinks, lots of ice.*, *فطور سينا*).
- **Work-friendly positioning:** "A place for every kind of work", "Made to fit your flow".
- Lowercase, short, relaxed. Friendly rather than formal.

---

## 11. Quick Design Recipe (to recreate the feel)

1. **Palette:** cream `#F1EBE0` background, rust `#8A4A2A` for primary marks, black for contrast.
2. **Logo style:** single-weight line art built from parallel strokes.
3. **Type pair:** condensed high-contrast serif (EN) + light Arabic sans with tatweel (AR) + tiny lowercase sans sub-lines.
4. **Texture:** tatreez borders, cross-stitch, engraving-style illustration, kraft, wood, stucco.
5. **Photos:** warm, natural light, close-ups, real people, lots of plants.
6. **Layout:** centered white text directly on photos with plenty of empty space and no decorative boxes.
